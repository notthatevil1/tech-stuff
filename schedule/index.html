Appointment request

Choose your preferred starting time.

Google shows a 60-minute request window. It is not the confirmed length of your service. Josh checks the full job length, travel, and other commitments before confirming.

Requesting the three-hour block? Choose your preferred start and enter Three-hour tech help block, $150 in the service field. The online request alone does not reserve three hours. Josh must confirm the full block.

Check the calendar’s timezone. Business hours are 9 AM to 9 PM Pacific. Google may display times in your browser’s timezone; follow the timezone shown inside the calendar.

Enter your name, email, phone number, service, and in-home or remote preference. For an in-home visit, give your city only. A brief problem description is optional.

Open the appointment calendar in a new tab

What happens after you press Book?

Your request goes to Google Calendar. Josh will call the number you provide to confirm the appointment, full job length, location, and expected cost. Until that call, treat the time as a request.

Need same-day help or a time you cannot find? Call (503) 662-2669 or text Josh.

Google hosts the booking calendar. Please do not enter passwords, security codes, payment details, or a full street address in the booking form. Privacy details.
