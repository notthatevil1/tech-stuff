What do you need help with?

Starting prices are shown before you choose a time.

Choose a service above. After you pick a time, enter that same service in the required booking field.

Published prices cover the named service. Equipment, wall conditions, travel, compatibility, and extra work can affect the final quote. Josh will explain and confirm costs before work begins.

Step 2

Pick an available time.

The calendar shows one-hour request times from 9 AM to 9 PM Pacific and hides conflicts already on Josh’s calendar. Choose a time, then enter your name, email, phone number, service, location preference, and a brief description.

This is an appointment request. Josh will call the number you provide to confirm the service, location, final time, and expected cost.
