The person behind Tech-Stuff

Hi, I’m Josh.

Local. Independent. Human.

When you call, you’re talking directly to the person who will actually be working on your problem.

Good help starts
with a real person.

I started Tech-Stuff because people shouldn’t need an IT department just to get good technical help.

I like figuring out why things aren’t working, finding practical solutions, and explaining technology in normal English.

You’re not getting shuffled through a corporate call center or pressured into buying something you don’t need.

My approach is simple:

  • Figure out what’s actually wrong.
  • Explain your options clearly.
  • Fix what makes sense.
  • Don’t sell you shit you don’t need.

Technology can be frustrating enough already.
Getting help with it shouldn’t be.
