Let’s find the actual problem.

A computer that keeps freezing doesn’t automatically need replacing. Software, storage, updates, and hardware can cause similar symptoms. I’ll work through the cause before recommending a repair or upgrade.

  • Slow desktops and laptops
  • Windows problems and software errors
  • Crashes, freezing, and random restarts
  • Startup problems and computers that won’t turn on
  • Drivers and connected devices
  • Storage space and drive problems
  • Hardware and performance troubleshooting
  • Printers and peripherals

Tell me what you notice, when it started, and any error message you can see. If you don’t have those details, that’s fine too. For a computer that won’t start or a suspected hardware problem, we’ll discuss the right way to look at it.

A few common questions

Can you help without coming to my home?

Some software, browser, and Windows issues can be investigated remotely if the computer starts and has an internet connection. Hardware and connection problems may need an in-home visit.

Should I repair or replace my computer?

That depends on the fault, the condition of the computer, and what you need it to do. I’ll explain the likely cost and give you options before major work begins.
