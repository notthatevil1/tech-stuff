A few common questions

Can you set up streaming apps and accounts?

Yes. I can help install and configure supported apps and devices. You’ll need access to your own accounts; streaming subscriptions are separate.

Do I need to buy a mount first?

Contact Josh with your TV model and wall details first if you’re unsure. The right mount depends on the screen and installation conditions.

Can you hide every cable inside the wall?

Cable routing depends on wall access and the cable type. Electrical work may need a licensed professional. We’ll discuss safe, suitable cable management options before installation.
