Windows 11 Readiness & Upgrades

Not sure whether your current computer should be upgraded or replaced? I’ll check what it supports and explain your options.

Check compatibility before spending money.

Windows 11 readiness involves hardware, firmware settings, and the software you rely on. I’ll look at those together, including the computer’s overall condition and what you need it to do.

  • Processor compatibility
  • TPM support
  • Secure Boot capability
  • BIOS or UEFI settings
  • Available storage
  • Memory
  • Drivers and connected equipment
  • Application compatibility and hardware condition

If an upgrade is suitable, we’ll discuss backups and preparation first. If it isn’t, I’ll explain the practical alternatives. Sometimes the right answer is to keep the computer you already have—with a clear understanding of its supported options.

A few common questions

Does every Windows computer support Windows 11?

No. Microsoft has specific hardware and security requirements. I’ll check your particular computer against the official requirements rather than assuming it is compatible.

Will an upgrade keep my files and applications?

That depends on the installation path and application compatibility. Important files should be backed up before an operating-system change. We’ll review that before starting.

Will you bypass the hardware requirements?

I focus on supported, practical upgrade options. If the computer does not meet Microsoft’s requirements, I’ll explain what that means and help compare sensible alternatives.
