A few common questions

Can you help with devices I already own?

Yes. Tell me the make and model and what you want to achieve. I’ll check compatibility and the existing setup before suggesting additional equipment.

Does camera setup include professional monitoring?

No professional monitoring service is included. Device setup and basic configuration are separate from any subscription or monitoring plan offered by a manufacturer.

Are installation prices fixed for every home?

The published prices are for the named services. Access, mounting, wiring, equipment, and additional work can affect the quote. I’ll confirm the scope and cost before work begins.
