A person, not a call center

Every problem
is a puzzle.

The important part is figuring out what actually makes sense for the person using the technology.

I enjoy troubleshooting because every problem is a puzzle. Sometimes the solution is replacing hardware. Sometimes it’s changing a setting. Sometimes it’s undoing something Windows decided to do all by itself at 2:00 in the morning.

That’s why Tech-Stuff exists. I wanted to create the kind of tech support I’d want someone I care about to receive.

Patient. Straightforward. Competent. No pressure.

No confusing jargon unless you actually want the technical explanation. And no pretending you need an expensive replacement when a simple repair will solve the problem.

What you can expect

I’ll listen first.

You usually know more about the problem than you think.

I’ll explain what I find.

You should understand what you’re paying to fix.

I’ll give you options.

Sometimes there is more than one reasonable solution.

I won’t make you feel stupid.

Technology is complicated. That’s why people like me have jobs.

I’ll tell you when something isn’t worth fixing.

Not every repair makes financial sense. I’d rather keep your trust than squeeze another job out of you.
